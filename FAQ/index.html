FAQs

What’s the dress code?
Whatever makes you feel good! There are no predetermined levels of fanciness or colors.
Wear sensible shoes, you will be walking across grass and gravel.
See the below question regarding weather and plan to bring (you guessed it) layers.

What weather should I expect?
The weather on the coast can be hard to predict. It could be hot, it could be perfect, and it could be fogged in all on the same day. All we know for sure is that you should bring layers. If you have a particular proclivity for getting cold you could even bring a blanket if you’d like, we will also aim to provide blankets and heat lamps should it get chilly.

What will happen in variable weather conditions?
All events will still be held outdoors as planned, with a tent and heat lamps on standby to keep things comfortable in a range of coastal weather patterns.

Where will the ceremony and reception take place? And will it all be outdoors?
All Sunday events will take place at Pie Ranch in Pescadero, CA. The ceremony and reception will be held outside.

What accessibility information about the venue is available?
Pie Ranch is a working farm with a variety of uneven surfaces. The ceremony and reception will be held in a grassy area with uneven surfaces very close to one another, about a 5 minute walk away from the upper parking area. No stair steps are required to access the area.
Our understanding is that getting to and from the ceremony and reception site is largely navigable with a walker or cane, and would be navigable but somewhat bumpy in a wheelchair.
For those needing additional accommodations, cars can drop people off closer to the ceremony and reception site on a limited basis. Additionally, the very kind people at Pie Ranch will be available with a golf cart to assist with transportation within the ranch for anyone who needs or wants it.
There will be accessible restrooms on site.

What meal options will there be for people with dietary restrictions?
Please provide us with your dietary restrictions in your RSVP and we’ll make sure there is plenty of food that you can eat! At this stage the meals have not yet been planned.

What parking is available?
There will be parking available on site at Pie Ranch on Sunday. Pie Ranch staff will help direct you to parking places. However, we strongly encourage you to carpool if possible (see spreadsheet here!).

Is there going to be a shuttle?
There will not be a shuttle. Please plan to Carpool or drive yourself.

Are kids invited?
Yes, human kids of are more than welcome! Your adult children will receive their own invitation if invited. Please reach out to us if you are unsure & we hope you understand. We love your dogs but please do not bring them (seriously, please don’t).

Reach out to Jacob and he can give you the intel.

Probably we will at some point, but we don’t have one yet (thanks for asking). Check back again later if you’d like.
