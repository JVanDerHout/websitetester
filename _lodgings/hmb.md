<strong>Half Moon Bay</strong> is about 40 minutes to the south of San Francisco, and about 25 minutes from Pescadero (a.k.a. Pie Ranch, the venue). <br>
Half Moon Bay has a range of hotels, bed & breakfasts, and short term rentals if you're looking for something closer to the venue while still being in a town and having access to the coast and San Francisco. <br>
<br>
