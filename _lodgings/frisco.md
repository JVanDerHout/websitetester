Travel to Pescadero is completely do-able as a day trip from the greater bay area. <br>
<br>
If you are traveling from out of town and want to stay in the greater bay area, we recommend <strong>San Francisco</strong> as the closest major city to Pescadero, about an hour and fifteen minutes away by car. SF has the most flight options if you're flying in, plus plenty of hotels and rentals across price ranges, and lots to do if you want to extend your trip into a mini vacation. Travel from the North Bay or East Bay may add on an additional half and hour or more. <br>
<br>
