Travel to Pescadero is completely do-able as a day trip from the greater bay area. <br>
<br>
If you are traveling from out of town and want to stay in the greater bay area, we recommend <strong>San Francisco</strong> as the closest major city to Pescadero, about an hour and fifteen minutes away by car. SF has the most flight options if you're flying in, plus plenty of hotels and rentals across price ranges, and lots to do if you want to extend your trip into a mini vacation. <br>
<br>
<strong>Half Moon Bay</strong> is another great option. Half Moon Bay is about 40 minutes to the south of San Francisco, and about 25 minutes from Pescadero (a.k.a. Pie Ranch, the venue). Half Moon Bay has a range of hotels, bed & breakfasts, and rentals if you're looking for something closer to the venue while still being in a town. <br>
<br>
<strong>Santa Cruz</strong> also has many options for places to stay. Santa Cruz is about an hour and a half south of SF and is located about 30 minutes south from Pescadero (a.k.a. Pie Ranch, the venue). Santa Cruz also has a range of hotel and rental options and is a fun place to explore. <br>
<br>
