Travel to Pescadero is completely do-able as a day trip from the greater bay area. <br>
If you are traveling from out of town and want to stay in the greater bay area, we recommend San Francisco as the closest city to Pescadero. <br>
Santa Cruz is also a lovely place to visit and there are plenty of hotel and rental options in a range of prices. <br>
