**<a href="www.costanoa.com">Costanoa</a>** <br>
  
Costanoa offers many different lodging options at a range of prices and is the closest hotel to the venue, located just a *four minute drive* away from Pie Ranch. Costanoa's lodging options include cabins, glamping, hotel-style lodge rooms, and camping. We don't know how long rooms will be available, so go ahead and grab a room If you want to stay at Costanoa.</p>

<p>There is no minimum number of nights you need to stay on a Sunday so if you just want to stay the night at the wedding, you can have a one night reservation.If you're coming early and plan to stay on Saturday, there is a two night minimum, so you can reserve for Saturday and Sunday nights.</p>

<p>Here is a brief summary of the rooms:</p>

<h2>1. Lodge rooms.</h2>
<p>Lodge rooms are the most like a typical hotel. Lodge rooms are the only rooms that have private bathrooms with showers.</p>

<h2>2. Douglas Fir cabins.</h2>
<p>There are just a few of these cabins, but these are very well reviewed. Thecabins have a half bath (toilet but no shower or tub). The units share a shower house which also has a sauna.</p>

<h2>3. Tent cabins (Bungalows)</h2>
<p>There are a number of tent cabins, which are also referred to as bungalows, with different bedding configurations.The tent cabins/bungalows do not have their own bathrooms or showers, but you share a bathhouse with toilets, showers and a sauna.</p>
<p>Check the website as there are quite a few options. The Outer Pine Village has queen beds. Pine Village has tent cabins with a double and a twin and some larger family set ups. The Lighthouse Bungalows include some family configurations with a double, trundle and bunk.</p>

<h2>4. RVs & Tent Camping</h2>
<p>There are a variety of sites that have back ins for RVs and also platforms for tents. They vary and you can call or read the fine print Multiple tents can be pitched on one site.</p>

**TLDR: There are lots of options but we don't have a hold on any rooms so book early if you can!**
