**<a href="www.costanoa.com">Costanoa</a>** <br>
  
Costanoa offers many different lodging options at a range of prices and is the closest hotel to the venue, located just a *four minute drive* away from Pie Ranch. Costanoa's lodging options include cabins, glamping, hotel-style lodge rooms, and camping. We don't know how long rooms will be available, so go ahead and grab a room If you want to stay at Costanoa.There is no minimum number of nights you need to stay on a Sunday so if you just want to stay the night at the wedding, you can have a one night reservation.If you're coming early and plan to stay on Saturday, there is a two night minimum, so you can reserve for Saturday and Sunday nights. <br>

<p>Here is a brief summary of the rooms:</p>

<h3>1. Lodge rooms.</h3>
<p>Lodge rooms are the most like a typical hotel. Lodge rooms are the only rooms that have private bathrooms with showers.</p>

<h3>2. Douglas Fir cabins.</h3>
<p>There are just a few of these cabins, but these are very well reviewed. The cabins have a half bath (toilet but no shower or tub). The units share a shower house which also has a sauna.</p>

<h3>3. Tent cabins (Bungalows)</h3>
<p>There are a number of tent cabins (bungalows). These don't have their own bathrooms or showers, but you have access to shared toilets, showers and a sauna. Options include queen beds, doubles and twin, and some with larger set ups (including trundles and bunks).</p>

<h3>4. RVs & Tent Camping</h3>
<p>Costanoa also has are sites that have back ins for RVs and platforms for tents. They vary and you can call or read the fine print Multiple tents can be pitched on one site.</p>

**TLDR: There are lots of options but we don't have a hold on any rooms so book early if you can!**
