**<a href="www.costanoa.com">Costanoa</a>** <br>
  
Costanoa offers many different lodging options at a range of prices and is located just a *four minute drive* away from Pie Ranch.Costanoa's lodging options include cabins, glamping, hotel-style lodge rooms, and camping. <br>

***Reservations***

<p>Costanoa reservations can be made on the Costanoa website, <a href="https://www.costanoa.com" target="_blank">https://www.costanoa.com</a>. The phone number is 650.879.1100.</p>

<p>Costanoa is the nearest resort/hotel to the wedding site, approximately 2 miles (a four minute drive) away. If you are coming to the wedding, we don't know how long rooms will be available, so go ahead and grab a room If you want to stay at Costanoa.</p>

<p>Check the cancellation policy when you book as it varies per room and dates but generally if you book directly on Costanoa's website (be careful as third party websites pop up on the internet) it usually is a 72 hours cancellation policy with a $20 cancellation fee. For exact prices, check the website.</p>

<p>There is no minimum number of nights you need to stay on a Sunday so if you just want to stay the night at the wedding, you can have a one night reservation.</p>

<p>If you're staying on a weekend, such as a Saturday, there is a two night minimum, so you can reserve for Saturday and Sunday nights, for the two night minimum.</p>

<p>Here is a brief summary of the rooms:</p>

<h2>1. Lodge rooms.</h2>
<p>All lodge rooms (North and South Lodge rooms) are the only rooms that have bathrooms with showers.</p>
<p>There are rooms with king beds ("King") and rooms with two double beds ("Double").</p>
<p>The Lodge rooms are on the first and second floors. There is no elevator in the lodge, so we try to book a first floor Lodge room if stairs are not a good idea for you.</p>
<p>Second floor rooms may have a slightly better view. There are view rooms and premium rooms. The premium rooms have a bigger balcony. More information is on the website.</p>

<h2>2. Douglas Fir cabins.</h2>
<p>There are just a few of these cabins, but these are very well reviewed as quiet and serene. These cabins have a half bath (toilet but no shower or tub). The units share a shower house which also has a sauna.</p>
<p>These are the only rooms with a half bath. They are double cabins, so you'll be sharing a wall with another room.</p>
<p>There are rooms with king beds ("King")and rooms with two double beds (Double).</p>

<h2>3. Tent cabins (Bungalows)</h2>
<p>There are a number of tent cabins, which are also referred to as bungalows, with different bedding configurations.</p>
<p>The tent cabins/bungalows do not have their own bathrooms or showers, but you share a bathhouse with toilets, showers and a sauna.</p>
<p>Check the website as there are quite a few options. The Outer Pine Village has queen beds. Pine Village has tent cabins with a double and a twin and some larger family set ups. The Lighthouse Bungalows include some family configurations with a double, trundle and bunk.</p>

<h2>4. Pull ins for RVs & tents</h2>
<p>There are a variety of sites that have back ins for RVs and also platforms for tents. They vary and you can call or read the fine print!</p>
<p>The Seascape Village is the most highly ranked. Some of the sites have ocean views, and they seem to be fairly large sites. RVs only here. There are better views but less shade here.</p>
<p>The Coastal Village allows RVs with a tent add-on. It's considered shaded. Check which sites allow the tent add-on.</p>
<p>The Whale North and Whale South are pull throughs for RVs. They have concrete or gravel pads. Some reviewers say that that the lots are pretty narrow and not as attractive as other areas.</p>
<p>The Eucalyptus Camp is shaded by eucalyptus trees and has back in and raised tent platforms. You can do a tent add-on. The even numbered campsites get better reviews than the odd numbered ones. It looks like these might have the smallest sites.</p>

<h2>5. Camping sites</h2>
<p>There are camp sites if you want to bring your own tent. Multiple tents can be pitched on one site.</p>
<p>The Lighthouse Grove tent village allows 2 to 6 tents per site. The Ohlone Grove Tent Village holds up to 2 tents and has a view.</p>

<p>There are lots of options but we don't have a hold on any rooms so book early if you can!</p>
