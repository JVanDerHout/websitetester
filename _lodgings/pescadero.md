Pescadero itself is a small, rural town. Aside from <a href="https://www.costanoa.com/">Costanoa Lodge</a> (see separate page in Lodgings section), options include short term rentals, an inn, a hostel, and camping. <br>
<br>
There are several short term rentals (aka Airbnb / VRBO options) in Pescadero and the surrounding coastal towns. Short term rentals right in town are limited and can be rustic or more on the "glamping" side of things. <br>
<br>
There is also a small inn called the <a href="https://www.pescaderocreekinn.com/">Pescadero Creek Inn</a>, located right in Pescadero. <br>
<br>
Additionally, there is a hostel in Pescadero which is very charming and comes with all the usual hostel experiences. <a href="https://www.hiusa.org/find-hostels/california/pescadero-210-pigeonpoint-road">More information on Pigeon Point Hostel here</a>. <br>
<br>
Please note: Ben Ries Campground in Butano State Park is currently closed due to fire related damage to the park's water system. <br>
<br>
A few things to keep in mind if you are looking at renting your own place: <br>
&emsp;• This area is popular on weekends and during summer, so we recommend booking as early as possible. <br>
&emsp;• Many rentals in this area are rural and rustic (more amenities are available a short drive away in Half Moon Bay or Santa Cruz). <br>
&emsp;• Some properties have minimum night stay requirements, especially on weekends. <br>
&emsp;• You may want to broaden your search from Half Moon Bay to Santa Cruz to increase your options. <br>
