There are several short term rentals (aka Airbnb / VRBO options) in Pescadero and the surrounding coastal towns (Half Moon Bay, Pacifica, San Gregorio, and Santa Cruz). <br>
<br>
Pescadero itself is a small, rural town, so rentals right in town are limited and can be rustic or more on the "glamping" side of things.
<br>
If you are interested in a short term rental along the coast we recommend looking in the radius from Half Moon Bay down to Santa Cruz.<br>
<br>
A few things to keep in mind if you are looking at renting your own place: <br>
&emsp;• This area is popular on weekends and during summer, so we recommend booking as early as possible. <br>
&emsp;• Many rentals in this area are rural and rustic (more amenities are available a short drive away in Half Moon Bay or Santa Cruz) <br>
&emsp;• Some properties have minimum-night-stay requirements, especially on weekends. <br>
