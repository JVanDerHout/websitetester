### Some recommendations for things to do in the area
## Food & Drink
Downtown Local, Pescadero: cafe and a nice store with fancy treats and local goodies <br>
Loma Mar Outpost, Loma Mar:  general store and cafe <br>
San Gregorio General Store, San Gregorio: historic general store with a bar and live music <br>
<a href="https://www.swantonberryfarm.com/">Swanton Berry Farm</a>, Davenport: U Pick strawberries, first organic farm in the country to sign a contract with the UFW <br>
<a href="https://bluehousefarm.com/">Blue House Farm</a>, Pescadero: U Pick strawberries and other seasonal produce <br>
{% include section.html %}
## Hiking & Beaches
Butano State Park, Pescadero: trails to explore <br>
Bean Hollow State Beach, Pescadero: tide pools and coastal walking paths <br>
Pescadero State Beach & Marsh Natural Preserve, Pescadero: sandy beach, walking trails, and wildlife viewing <br>
Pomponio State Beach, Pescadero: sandy beach for walking and relaxing <br>
{% include section.html %}
## Sights & Wildlife
<a href="https://www.parks.ca.gov/PigeonPoint">Pigeon Point Lighthouse</a>, Pescadero: one of the tallest lighthouses on the West Coast, free admission and parking, grounds open daily from 8am to sunset <br>
Año Nuevo State Park, Pescadero: home to northern elephant seals (though we'll be outside of peak season) <br>
<a href="https://www.harleyfarms.com/">Harley Farms Goat Dairy</a>, Pescadero, working goat farm with a cheese shop, open daily 11am to 4pm, tours available by reservation <br>
